CAREBRIDGE HOSPITALS - OUTPATIENT NO-SHOW PREDICTION MODEL EVALUATION
DS Sprint Skill-Based Assessment 2 | Challenge ID: U23AD491
===========================================================================

1. TARGET VARIABLE DEFINITION
   - Positive Class (1): No-show (patient did not attend scheduled visit)
   - Negative Class (0): Attended (patient completed appointment)
   - Unresolved, cancelled, rescheduled, and pending records were excluded from
     binary training to maintain clinical label integrity.

2. SPLIT METHODOLOGY
   - Strategy: Strict chronological train-test split (80% train, 20% test).
   - Training set size: 3576 (approx)
   - Test set size: 894
   - Temporal structure strictly preserved to mimic real-world deployment.

4. CHAMPION MODEL SELECTION RATIONALE
   The 'Logistic Regression' model was selected as the operational champion.
   In outpatient appointment scheduling, false negatives (failing to identify an upcoming
   no-show) lead to idle physician time and lost clinic capacity. Therefore, the selection
   prioritizes balanced F1-score and ROC-AUC over simple accuracy.

5. CONFUSION MATRIX (CHAMPION MODEL)
   True Attended  (TN): 425   | False No-show (FP): 248  
   False Attended (FN): 99    | True No-show  (TP): 122  

6. KEY PREDICTIVE SIGNALS
   - Booking Lead Time (waiting_days): Long lead times (>7 days) correlate with higher no-show risk.
   - Prior Patient History (prior_noshow_count & prior_noshow_rate): The single strongest predictor.
   - Reminder Status (reminder_sent & reminder_response): Confirmed reminders dramatically reduce risk.
   - Age Cohort & Departmental variations provide actionable secondary signals.
